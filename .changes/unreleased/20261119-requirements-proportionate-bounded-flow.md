- **The requirements flow produced documents far larger than the brief, and its single correction batch introduced new defects.** The requirements guide now asks the Lead and authors to resolve only the choices a source clause cannot be tested without, keep one requirement per source clause when the brief asks for the smallest contract, show coverage as ID lists instead of computed counts, and write drafts that stay consistent when copied (plain-text paths, clock-based timestamps, pass targets over runnable criteria). The correction batch fixes every RISK finding and any other finding only when it corrects a contradiction or an untestable or untraceable statement, by targeted edits, records each disposition once in the builder state and re-reads every passage it edited and each statement the edit may have made untrue (counts, ranges, dates, statuses, owners, scope claims such as "nothing is added", equivalence claims) against the final BRD, adding a criterion for each case a widened rule now reaches. A source clause that cannot be tested gets the requirement and criterion that test it rather than a recorded gap, and a conflict with a literal source clause is settled by removing or rewording what departs from it and what derives from it, since a provisional or unresolved label does not make both statements hold; any open part becomes one sponsor-owned question worded the same in both documents. The BRD author also receives the PRD capture so no finding is deferred to a batch that has closed, and each lead decision names the clause that cannot be tested without it. The coordinator creates the dated research, plans and reviews folders and the builder session folders before the first dispatch, so a generated document is not lost to a missing parent folder (`squad-src/.github/skills/squad/references/requirements-quality.md`, `tests/squad-behavior-contract.md`).
