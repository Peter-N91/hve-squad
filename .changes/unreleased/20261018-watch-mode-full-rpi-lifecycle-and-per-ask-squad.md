- **Watch Mode now selects a sub-squad per case and per ask and executes the complete RPI lifecycle across all triggers (#176).** `squad-watch-mode.instructions.md`, `references/federation.md`, `squad-watch.workflow.yml`, `.github/workflows/squad-watch.yml`, and `.github/workflows/sync-hve-core.yml` now require every Watch Mode event (`issues`, `pull_request`, `issue_comment`, `schedule`, `workflow_dispatch`, `push`) to seed its event sub-squad from the canonical templates with a profile and packs tailored to the case and ask (always including the full `researcher`, `lead`, `developer`, `tester`, `scribe` spine rather than copying a prior `members/*` roster), repair a missing federation root when `members/*` directories exist on disk without `federation.md`, and run the full Research → Plan → Council → Implement → Review pipeline via `runSubagent`/`task` dispatches (including on Sync and Adapt adaptation briefs). The workflow now checkpoints each completed role through Squad Scribe before dispatching the next stage, provisions the installed squad and `hve-core` package through a transactional ownership-tracked overlay, hands patches to the write-capable job from their exact producing checkout, and binds material handoff to a hash-pinned authorized-event baseline plus current structured state, stage, history, council, Scribe, and member-owned deliverable evidence while preserving legitimate no-change, resume, and conditional-Council outcomes. The handoff module now ships with the squad skill, and the reference trigger template explicitly names the extra credential-separation and write-job wiring required before an installed consumer can claim the same deterministic enforcement.
- **Watch Mode accepts ordinary fractional event timestamps and successful zero-failure test reports without weakening completion gates.** Material handoff preserves timestamp precision across baseline serialization, rejects unfinished results and heading-only plain-text evidence, enforces the Council's most-restrictive findings even when the summary claims a skip, and requires conditional findings to be resolved or explicitly accepted before the final patch.
- **Watch Mode serializes one repository delivery at a time, pins an immutable source SHA, and reconciles publication without re-interpreting a completed run.** Both workflows use `queue: max`. The live workflow fails closed on fork, non-bot, or unauthorized issue-to-PR mapping. The shipped template rejects a fork comment and does not claim that same issue-to-PR mapping. Validated current-run Open Questions are encoded by the trusted renderer before they reach a draft body or a numeric source-thread comment. That comment renders the staging artifact only after its member, run, thread, and trusted base match the resolver. The artifact is produced in the same read-only job as the model and is not independently attested. A free-form `workflow_dispatch` with no numeric thread records that outcome in the run summary and does not call `gh issue comment`. A run with no material change and no existing deliverable does not open a branch or pull request. Existing mapped branches accept tracking-only commits, commit `watch-publication.json` for orphan recovery, and stay fast-forward-only.
