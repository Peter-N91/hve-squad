- **Squad and Federation coordination move deterministic work out of model prompts.** Compact runtime contracts and artifact-scoped cold instructions replace broad startup reads. Canonical Init payload preparation, confirmed direct single-squad and federation initialization/expansion, sealed additive catalog-role/pack expansion, dependency-wave planning, actual-turn completion checks, ordinary federation persistence, and sealed-plan single-squad promotion use scripts, preserving existing state formats, native role dispatch, approval gates, routing modes and no-runtime fallbacks. Federation Init validates every new member through the standard Init builder and stages member transactions in parallel. Promotion inventories every candidate read-only, requires an exact plan hash and explicit relocate/keep decision, preserves the adopted member's ledger and run totals because the transaction spends no model credits, stages and ledger-checks the final federation, then publishes under the ordinary writer lock. Roster expansion resolves confirmed shipped rows and newly eligible canonical routes for a plain squad or one registered member, requires the exact plan hash, and writes only roster/routing/decision bytes while preserving consumption, state, rates, notifications and history byte-for-byte; no Scribe call means no fallback estimate/observed ledger rewrite.
